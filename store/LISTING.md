## Full description
Halo is a private place for you and your friends. No feeds, no ads, no strangers.
- Chat with text, voice notes and photos
- Voice and video calls that look and feel like Halo
- Moments and stories that stay for 24 hours
- Streaks and rings for the friends you talk to most
- Listen together: share a song and friends can join in
- Halo Music built in
- Block and report anyone, and delete your account any time in Settings
