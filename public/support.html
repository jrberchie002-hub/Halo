Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Account, then Delete account.

How do I block or report someone?

Open the chat menu with the three dots or a long press on the chat, then choose Block or Report.

I cannot log in.

Check your username and password. If you are locked out, write to us from any email with your username.
