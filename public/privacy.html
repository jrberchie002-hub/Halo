Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Halo is a private chat app for you and your friends. This page explains what we keep and why. Halo is run by Kora Technology (Berchy).

What we collect

  • Your account: username, display name, password (stored hashed, never readable), profile photo and status you choose to set.
  • Your content: messages, voice notes, photos, moments and stories, playlists and songs you share, and call signalling needed to connect calls.
  • Friends and requests, blocks and reports you make.
  • Basic technical data such as device type and approximate activity time, used to keep the service working and to prevent abuse.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not read your chats for marketing.

Who can see your content

Only the friends you chat with, and people you choose to share to. Calls connect directly between devices where possible.

Services we use

Hosting and database providers store your data on our behalf. Music search and playback use third party services such as YouTube; their own privacy terms apply to that playback.

Permissions

Camera, microphone, photos and notifications are used only when you start a call, record, attach media or turn alerts on. You can turn them off in your device settings.

Keeping and deleting

You can hide or clear chats, and set how long messages are kept. You can delete your account in Settings, under Account, at any time. This removes your profile, friends, chats, stories and shared music from our servers. Copies held by friends in their own devices are not under our control.

Safety

You can block and report anyone from the chat menu. Reports are reviewed by us and may lead to removal of an account.

Children

Halo is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
